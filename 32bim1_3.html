Section 1: Reading Comprehension

Read the text carefully and complete the activities below.

Santino Morales is fifteen years old and he comes from Bariloche, although he now lives in the neighbourhood of Villa Urquiza, in Buenos Aires. Every morning, he rides his bike ten minutes to his bilingual secondary school. Santino is a very curious and reliable student. He loves astronomy and chess, and he prefers reading science magazines to playing video games. In his spare time, he builds telescope models and can speak German quite well, though he doesn't enjoy swimming in cold water.

Last Thursday was a memorable evening for Santino and his school astronomy club. At eight o'clock, they visited the Planetario Galileo Galilei in Palermo with their Physics teacher. They watched a projection about the Milky Way and observed Saturn through powerful telescopes on the terrace. During the activity, Santino took detailed notes and asked the astronomer three questions about black holes.

After the observation session, the group didn't take the train back immediately; instead, they drank hot chocolate near the lake in Bosques de Palermo. Unfortunately, Santino forgot his umbrella on a bench when it started to drizzle, so he arrived home at half past eleven completely wet, yet thrilled by the experience.
