Section 1: Reading Comprehension

Read the text carefully and complete the activities below.

Delfina Cabrera is a sixteen-year-old violinist from Tucumán, though she has studied and lived in San Telmo, Buenos Aires, since 2023. Delfina is a dedicated young musician. She loves chamber music and jazz, and she prefers practising in acoustic halls to playing in noisy auditoriums. In her spare time, she composes short melodies and can read complex orchestral scores effortlessly.

Since moving to Buenos Aires, Delfina has performed in four youth orchestra concerts, and she has already recorded a solo piece for a cultural radio programme. Last month, she auditioned for the National Youth Symphony at the Teatro Colón, where she earned the position of first violin. According to her conductor, Delfina is the most expressive violinist in the ensemble and much more confident than she was last year.

The annual gala concert will take place next Saturday. If Delfina performs well at the gala, she will receive an invitation to tour Europe with the orchestra. As soon as the concert finishes, she will call her grandmother in Tucumán. As Delfina always believes, when musicians practise with passion, they move the audience's heart.
